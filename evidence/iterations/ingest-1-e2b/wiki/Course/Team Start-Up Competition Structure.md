# Team Start-Up Competition Structure

This describes the team project structure, which involves adopting a real early-stage startup, researching it, interviewing the founder, and pitching it at Demo Day. It details the required roles and the timeline for key deliverables.

## Key points
- Teams of 3–4 adopt a real early-stage startup, research it, interview its founder, and pitch it at Demo Day. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- The arc includes picking a startup, completing the founder interview, drafting a pitch deck, and attending Demo Days. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- Four roles are chosen by consensus: Team Lead, Industry Lead, Finance Lead, and Marketing & Tech Lead. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- The founder interview is a graded checkpoint and a practice run for Demo Day. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
