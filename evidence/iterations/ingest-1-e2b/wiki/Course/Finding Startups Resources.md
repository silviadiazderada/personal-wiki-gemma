# Finding Startups Resources

This outlines the specific platforms and databases students can use to find potential startups, including directories, portfolios, and research tools.

## Key points
- Y Combinator provides a company directory filterable by Status = Active, Batch = recent, Industry, and Location = SF Bay Area. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Berkeley SkyDeck portfolio is useful for finding Berkeley-affiliated founders. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Pitchbook can be used to confirm stage, age, and HQ, with a limit of ≤ 10 rows/day and ≤ 25 rows/month. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 6)
