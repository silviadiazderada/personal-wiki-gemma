# First Job Advice

This advice focuses on the nature of a first job, internships, and building professional relationships.

## Key points
- A first job is just a first job and is not life-defining. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 3)
- Internships are valid entry points, even after graduation. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 3)
- Interviews test fit, and the real question is whether you can see yourself working with the person every day. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 3)
- Build relationships authentically by getting to know people before asking about their jobs. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 3)
