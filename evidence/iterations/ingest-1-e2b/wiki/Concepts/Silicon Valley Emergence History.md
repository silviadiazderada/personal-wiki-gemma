# Silicon Valley Emergence History

This section traces the historical development of Silicon Valley, showing how it evolved from an orchard region to a federally funded research and defense economy.

## Key points
- ~80% of Fairchild's business was government contracts. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Vannevar Bush's Office of Scientific Research and Development poured federal money into university labs. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 6)
- Lockheed Missiles & Space moved to Sunnyvale in 1956 and was the Valley's largest employer for decades. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 6)
- Semiconductors spun out of that ecosystem: Shockley (1956) → Fairchild (1957) → Intel (1968). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 6)
