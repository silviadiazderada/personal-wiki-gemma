# Legal System Impact

The legal system in the Valley developed a specific structure by having firms take equity instead of fees and by California's stance on non-competes.

## Key points
- Valley firms took equity instead of fees from cash-poor startups, creating a risk mindset. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
- California's refusal to enforce non-competes keeps talent moving, allowing ideas to cross-pollinate. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slides 5, 15)
