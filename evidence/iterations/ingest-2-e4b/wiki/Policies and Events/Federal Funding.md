# Federal Funding

Federal and military money has been a primary driver of US innovation for decades, forming the foundation of the tech economy.

## Key points
- Federal and military money drove US innovation for 80 years, starting with WWII and the Cold War. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Sputnik (1957) triggered a surge in funding through NASA, ARPA, and the National Defense Education Act (1958). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 6)
- In 1958, approximately 80% of Fairchild's business was government contracts. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
