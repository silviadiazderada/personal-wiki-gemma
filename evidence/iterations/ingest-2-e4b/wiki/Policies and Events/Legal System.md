# Legal System

The Valley's legal system supports innovation by allowing firms to take equity instead of fees and by not enforcing non-compete agreements.

## Key points
- Valley law firms built the scaffolding for startups by taking equity instead of fees. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- California's refusal to enforce non-competes allows engineers to job-hop, promoting talent cross-pollination. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
