# Venture Capital

Venture capital is a form of funding that allows high-risk ventures to survive by taking equity and absorbing potential losses.

## Key points
- Venture capital financed the un-financeable by taking equity and absorbing losses when a company failed. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 13)
- The federal SBIC program (1958) leveraged private risk capital to seed early Valley firms. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Venture capital has become an insular 'old-boys network' with limited diversity. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 13)
