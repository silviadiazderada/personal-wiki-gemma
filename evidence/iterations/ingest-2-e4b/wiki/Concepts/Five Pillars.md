# Five Pillars

The Five Pillars are the five key factors that shaped and maintain Silicon Valley's competitive edge over the long term.

## Key points
- The five pillars are: The state / government policy, Research universities, Venture capital, Immigrants, and The legal system. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- The state/government policy drove US innovation through WWII, the Cold War, and federal funding like ARPA and NDEA. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Research universities supply the people, with Stanford noted for its applied-engineering culture and Research Park. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Venture capital provides funding for ventures that banks would not touch, often taking equity. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Immigrants constitute a significant portion of high-tech firms and Bay Area residents. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- California's legal system supports talent mobility by refusing to enforce non-competes. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
