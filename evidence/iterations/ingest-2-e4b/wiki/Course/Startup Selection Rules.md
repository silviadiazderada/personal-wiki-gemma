# Startup Selection Rules

Startup selection must be made by consensus and adhere to specific criteria regarding funding stage, founding location, and age.

## Key points
- A startup must be pre–Series A, founded in Silicon Valley (SF / East Bay), no more than 3 years old, and have only received seed funding (Slide 4). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
- Startups can be found through Y Combinator, Berkeley SkyDeck, or a16z Speedrun (Slide 4). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
- Teams must claim their chosen company in the shared Google Sheet on a first-come, first-served basis (Slide 4). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
