# Founder Interview Process

The founder interview is a graded component requiring team participation and a formal presentation deliverable.

## Key points
- The interview should ideally be with the founder, but interviewing anyone working in Silicon Valley tech or a tech-adjacent role is acceptable (Slide 7). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- The interview must be 30–60 minutes, conducted on Zoom or in person, with every team member present (Slide 7). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- The deliverable requires 3 slides and a 5–7 minute presentation with equal speaking time (Slide 7). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
