# Founder Outreach Strategy

Students must proactively reach out to founders using professional methods, as responses can take several weeks.

## Key points
- Founders are busy, and replies can take 2–3 weeks, necessitating the lining up of a backup person early (Slide 8). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
- Methods for reaching founders include using SkyDeck company pages, LinkedIn with a specific note, and the YC company page (Slide 8). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
- The outreach email must be professional, stating who the students are, why they chose the company, proposing 30–45 minutes on Zoom, and listing all team members (Slide 8). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
