# Professional Profile Building

Building a professional profile involves taking various steps to gain experience and build a narrative for career advancement.

## Key points
- Earn a certification or take a course ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Join or lead a Berkeley club or initiative ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Enter a competition or hackathon ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Apply to scholarships or fellowships in the field ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Look for company challenges and initiatives, not only job posts ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Do pro bono work or help a nonprofit in your field ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Attend or organize industry events ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
- Take a short internship or project in an industry company ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 5)
