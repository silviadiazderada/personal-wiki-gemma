# Startup Research Resources

Several platforms are provided to help students research and verify details about potential startups.

## Key points
- Y Combinator's company directory can be filtered by Status, Batch, Industry, and Location = SF Bay Area (Slide 5). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Berkeley SkyDeck is a resource for Berkeley-affiliated founders, referencing current Batch and Pad-13 cohorts (Slide 5). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Pitchbook is a database used to confirm a company's stage, age, and HQ, with limits of ≤ 10 rows/day and ≤ 25 rows/month for UCB students (Slide 6). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 6)
