# Counterculture Thread

The 1960s Bay Area was the center of the American counterculture, which fused with the defense industry to create a paradox where anti-establishment ethos became the marketing for powerful industries.

## Key points
- In the 1960s the Bay Area was the center of the American counterculture — the Berkeley Free Speech Movement (1964), anti-Vietnam protest, Haight-Ashbury, communes, a deep distrust of big institutions: government, the military, and corporations like IBM. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 14)
- That rebel, anti-establishment ethos became the self-image and the marketing of what is now one of the most powerful, concentrated industries on earth. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 14)
