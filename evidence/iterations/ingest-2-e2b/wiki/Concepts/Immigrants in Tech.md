# Immigrants in Tech

Immigrants have become a significant part of the tech workforce, with a growing share in high-tech firms and a role in talent pipelines.

## Key points
- ~25% of US high-tech firms had an immigrant founder; ~40% of Valley firms by the internet era. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
- ~44% of Bay Area residents are foreign-born; ~38% of AI engineers are Chinese-born. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
- Berkeley and Stanford are the main talent pipelines; fully-funded STEM PhDs draw top talent on merit. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
