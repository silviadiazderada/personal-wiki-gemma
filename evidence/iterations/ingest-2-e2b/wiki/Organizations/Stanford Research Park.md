# Stanford Research Park

Stanford Research Park was created by Fred Terman to lease land it could not sell, creating a working loop between technology firms and the university.

## Key points
- Land it could not sell. Stanford held a large endowment of land it was barred from selling; Fred Terman's answer was to lease it — creating Stanford Research Park (1951), the first university-owned industrial park. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 8)
