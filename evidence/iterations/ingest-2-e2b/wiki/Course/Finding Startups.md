# Finding Startups

This section details the resources available for finding potential startups, such as Y Combinator, Berkeley SkyDeck, and a16z Speedrun, along with Pitchbook for research.

## Key points
- Y Combinator — company directory — ycombinator.com/companies filter by Status = Active, Batch = recent, Industry, and Location = SF Bay Area ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Berkeley SkyDeck — portfolio — skydeck.berkeley.edu/portfolio Berkeley-affiliated founders; look at the current Batch and Pad-13 cohorts ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- a16z Speedrun — a16z.com/speedrun games, consumer, and AI startups backed by Andreessen Horowitz ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Pitchbook — library.berkeley.edu → databases → Pitchbook use it to confirm stage, age, and HQ — and later for funding comparables ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 6)
