# Startup Selection Rules

Rules dictate the criteria for selecting a startup, including its funding stage, founding location, and age, which must be determined through team consensus.

## Key points
- It must be: pre–Series A · founded in Silicon Valley (SF / East Bay) · no more than 3 years old · seed funding only. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
