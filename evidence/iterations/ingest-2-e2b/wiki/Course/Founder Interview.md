# Founder Interview

The founder interview is a graded checkpoint where team members must conduct a 30–60 minute call with the founder, requiring preparation and specific deliverables.

## Key points
- 30–60 minutes, on Zoom or in person. EVERY team member must be on the call. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- Take a screenshot with all members visible — it has to appear on your slides for full credit. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- Deliverable: 3 slides, a 5–7 minute presentation with equal speaking time. Slides due ~10/18; presentations 10/27–10/30. Worth 5%. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
