# Team Start-Up Competition

The team start-up competition involves adopting a real early-stage startup, researching it, interviewing its founder, and pitching it at Demo Day. This is worth 20% of the course grade plus the interview component.

## Key points
- In teams of 3–4 you adopt a real early-stage startup, research it, interview its founder, and pitch it at Demo Day. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- Worth 20% of the course grade (plus the interview, 5%). ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- The arc: Pick your startup → Start-Up Proposal (Team Lead, Google Form) due 10/11 → Founder interview + 3-slide presentation · present 10/27–10/30 → Pitch-deck draft to Prof. Chang · early November → Demo Days 11/13, 11/17, 11/19, 11/20 → Final Competition 12/1 → Reflection 12/4 ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- Four roles, chosen by consensus: Team Lead · Industry Lead · Finance Lead (your Pitchbook expert) · Marketing & Tech Lead. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
