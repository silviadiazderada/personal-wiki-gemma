# Founder Outreach

Students are advised to begin outreach immediately due to the time founders take to respond, and they should use professional methods to contact founders.

## Key points
- Founders can take 2–3 weeks to reply, so teams should line up a backup person early. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
- Methods for reaching founders include SkyDeck company pages, LinkedIn, and the YC company page. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
- Emails must be professional, stating who the student is, the class, why they chose the company, and proposing two specific times for a 30–45 minute Zoom call. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
