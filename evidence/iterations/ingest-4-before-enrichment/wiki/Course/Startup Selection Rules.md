# Startup Selection Rules

Startups must meet specific criteria to be eligible for the competition, including being pre-Series A, founded in Silicon Valley, and no more than three years old.

## Key points
- A startup must be pre–Series A, founded in Silicon Valley (SF / East Bay), no more than 3 years old, and have only seed funding. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
- Startups can be found through Y Combinator, Berkeley SkyDeck, or a16z Speedrun. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
- Teams must claim their chosen startup in the shared Google Sheet on a first-come, first-served basis. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
