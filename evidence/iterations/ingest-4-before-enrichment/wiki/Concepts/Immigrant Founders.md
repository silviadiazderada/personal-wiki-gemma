# Immigrant Founders

Immigrant founders are one of the five pillars of Silicon Valley: immigrants come for education, often through Berkeley and Stanford, and a large share go on to found companies.

## Key points
- Immigrants have for decades come to Silicon Valley for education and become company founders. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 8)
- About 25% of US high-tech firms had an immigrant founder, and about 40% of Valley firms by the internet era. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
- About 44% of Bay Area residents are foreign-born; about 38% of AI engineers are Chinese-born. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
- The vulnerability: tightening student visas and work authorization directly benefits China's AI sector. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
