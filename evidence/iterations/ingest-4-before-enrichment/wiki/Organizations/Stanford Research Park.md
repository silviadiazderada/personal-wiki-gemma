# Stanford Research Park

Stanford Research Park is the first university-owned industrial park, created by leasing land that the university was barred from selling.

## Key points
- Fred Terman created the Stanford Research Park (1951) by leasing land the university could not sell. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 8)
- The Research Park fostered a working loop where companies recruited students and funded research from the university. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 8)
- Fred Terman promoted an applied-engineering culture at Stanford to serve the military-industrial complex. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 8)
