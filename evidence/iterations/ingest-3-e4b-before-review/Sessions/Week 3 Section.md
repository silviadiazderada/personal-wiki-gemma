# Week 3 Section

This source outlines the logistics and requirements for the Team Start-Up Competition in POLECON 156, focusing heavily on the process of selecting a startup and conducting a founder interview. It details deadlines, team roles, and specific resources for finding and contacting early-stage companies.

## Topics covered
- [[Founder Interview Process]]: The founder interview is a graded checkpoint where the entire team must participate in a 30–60 minute discussion with the startup's founder or a tech professional.
- [[Founder Outreach Strategy]]: Students are advised to begin outreach immediately due to the time founders take to respond, and they should use professional methods to contact founders.
- [[Startup Research Resources]]: Several online platforms are provided to help students research and verify the details of potential startups.
- [[Startup Selection Rules]]: Startups must meet specific criteria to be eligible for the competition, including being pre-Series A, founded in Silicon Valley, and no more than three years old.
- [[Team Start-Up Competition]]: The Team Start-Up Competition requires students to adopt a real early-stage startup, interview its founder, and pitch it at Demo Day, accounting for 20% of the course grade.
