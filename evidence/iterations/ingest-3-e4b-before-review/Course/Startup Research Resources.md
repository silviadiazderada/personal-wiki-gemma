# Startup Research Resources

Several online platforms are provided to help students research and verify the details of potential startups.

## Key points
- Y Combinator directory can be filtered by Status = Active, Batch = recent, Industry, and Location = SF Bay Area. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Berkeley SkyDeck portfolio lists Berkeley-affiliated founders, referencing current Batch and Pad-13 cohorts. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 5)
- Pitchbook is a database used to confirm a startup's stage, age, and HQ, with limits of ≤ 10 rows/day and ≤ 25 rows/month for UCB students. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 6)
