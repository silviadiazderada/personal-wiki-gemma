# Team Start-Up Competition

The Team Start-Up Competition requires students to adopt a real early-stage startup, interview its founder, and pitch it at Demo Day, accounting for 20% of the course grade.

## Key points
- The competition is worth 20% of the course grade, plus 5% for the founder interview. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- The competition arc includes picking a startup, submitting a Start-Up Proposal due 10/11, and presenting the founder interview on 10/27–10/30. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- Teams must choose four roles: Team Lead, Industry Lead, Finance Lead, and Marketing & Tech Lead. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
- Demo Days are scheduled for 11/13, 11/17, 11/19, and 11/20, with the Final Competition on 12/1. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 3)
