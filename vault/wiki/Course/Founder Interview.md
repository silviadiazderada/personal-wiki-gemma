# Founder Interview

The founder interview is a graded checkpoint where the entire team participates in a 30–60 minute discussion with the startup's founder or a tech professional.

## Key points
- The interview should be 30–60 minutes, conducted on Zoom or in person, with every team member present. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- The deliverable requires 3 slides and a 5–7 minute presentation with equal speaking time. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- Teams should prepare 4–5 core questions, with one person moderating and one taking notes. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 7)
- Suggested starting points include asking how the Berkeley ecosystem helped build the company or walking through the fundraising process. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 9)
- Each team member must ask at least one question, and the team must assign a moderator and a notetaker before the call. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 9)
