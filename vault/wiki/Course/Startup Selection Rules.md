# Startup Selection Rules

Startups chosen for the competition must meet specific criteria, including being pre-Series A, founded in Silicon Valley, and no more than three years old.

## Key points
- The startup must be pre–Series A, founded in Silicon Valley (SF / East Bay), no more than 3 years old, and at seed funding only. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
- Startups can be found through Y Combinator, Berkeley SkyDeck, or a16z Speedrun. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
- Teams must claim a startup in the shared Google Sheet, operating on a first come, first served basis. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 4)
