# Professional Profile Building

Building a professional profile involves taking proactive steps to gain experience and demonstrate capability.

## Key points
- Middle steps that build your profile include earning a certification or taking a course, joining or leading a Berkeley club or initiative, entering a competition or hackathon, applying to scholarships or fellowships in the field, looking for company challenges and initiatives, doing pro bono work or helping a nonprofit in your field, attending or organizing industry events, and taking a short internship or project in an industry company.
