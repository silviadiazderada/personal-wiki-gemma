# Founder Outreach

Effective outreach to busy founders requires a professional, targeted approach, and students must begin this process immediately.

## Key points
- Founders can be reached via SkyDeck company pages, LinkedIn with a specific note, or the YC company page founder profiles. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
- The outreach email must be professional, stating who the students are, why they chose the company, and proposing two specific times for a 30–45 minute Zoom call. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
- It is advised to line up a backup person early because founder replies can take 2–3 weeks. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week3_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 3 Section]], slide 8)
