# Fred Terman

Fred Terman is recognized as the 'Father of Silicon Valley' for establishing the model of linking academia with industry at Stanford.

## Key points
- Fred Terman ran a wartime research lab and returned determined to tie Stanford to defense work. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 6)
- Terman created Stanford Research Park (1951), the first university-owned industrial park. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 8)
- He brought the MIT military/industry model to Stanford, fostering an applied-engineering culture. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 12)
