# Vannevar Bush

Vannevar Bush was instrumental in establishing permanent federal funding for scientific research in the United States.

## Key points
- Vannevar Bush's Office of Scientific Research and Development poured federal money into university labs during WWII. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 6)
- He authored 'Science, the Endless Frontier' (1945) to establish permanent federal science funding. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 12)
