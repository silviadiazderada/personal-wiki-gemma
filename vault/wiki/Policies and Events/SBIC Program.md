# SBIC Program

The Small Business Investment Company (SBIC) program was a federal initiative that leveraged private capital to fund early high-risk ventures.

## Key points
- The 1958 Small Business Investment Act created the Small Business Investment Company program. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 13)
- The SBIC program leveraged private risk capital, lending roughly $2–$3 of low-cost government money for every $1 of private capital. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 13)

## Related notes
- [[Venture Capital]]: the SBIC multiplied the early risk-capital pool and seeded the Valley's first VC firms.
- [[Government Funding]]: the SBIC is federal money leveraged through private funds.
