# Non-Compete Agreements

Non-compete agreements are not enforced in California (a full ban since 2024), which keeps engineers moving between firms.

## Key points
- California's refusal to enforce non-competes keeps talent moving and allows ideas to cross-pollinate. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slides 5, 15)
- California has a full ban on non-competes since 2024, unlike New York where they are still enforceable. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
- California's non-enforcement of non-competes enabled talent mobility. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week5_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 5 Section]], slide 7)
