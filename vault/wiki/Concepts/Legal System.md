# Legal System

California's legal framework supports tech innovation through Valley law firms that take equity instead of fees and through California's refusal to enforce non-compete agreements (a full ban since 2024).

## Key points
- Valley law firms built the scaffolding for startups by taking equity instead of fees, similar to venture capital. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slides 5, 15)
- California's refusal to enforce non-competes allows engineers to job-hop, promoting the cross-pollination of ideas. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slides 5, 15)
- The same law firms may draft term sheets for investors and the startup, creating an inherent conflict of interest. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 15)
