# Five Pillars of Silicon Valley

The five pillars of Silicon Valley (the state, research universities, venture capital, immigrants and the legal system) are the factors that have shaped and maintained Silicon Valley's competitive edge over the long term.

## Key points
- The state: war and great-power competition have driven US innovation for 80 years, and federal and military money built the foundation. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Research universities supply the people, with Stanford noted for its applied-engineering culture and Research Park. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Venture capital provides funding for ventures that banks would not touch, often taking equity. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- Immigrants constitute a significant portion of high-tech firms and Bay Area residents. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
- The legal system in California supports innovation by refusing to enforce non-competes. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 5)
