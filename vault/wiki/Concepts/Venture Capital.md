# Venture Capital

Venture capital is a form of investment that finances high-risk startups by taking equity and absorbing potential losses, which was initially supported by government programs.

## Key points
- Georges Doriot is cited as the first venture capitalist, operating ARDC (1946) and funding DEC. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 12)
- The 1958 Small Business Investment Act created the SBIC program: the government licensed private investment funds and lent roughly $2–$3 of low-cost government money for every $1 of private capital. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slides 5, 13)
- Venture capital has become an insular 'old-boys network' where insider connections dictate deal flow. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 13)
