# Stanford Research Park

Stanford Research Park is the first university-owned industrial park, created by leasing land that Stanford was barred from selling.

## Key points
- Stanford held a large endowment of land it was barred from selling, which Terman leased to create the Research Park in 1951. ([[raw/POLECON156_Week2_DiscussionSection.pptx|Week 2 Section]], slide 8)
